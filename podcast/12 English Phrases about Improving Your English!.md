# other
## to make progress
- to move toward a goal or improve
- I am making progress in my English every day by practice.
- She has made significant progress in her vocabulary over the last few weeks.
- We are making good progress on our new project.
- Despite the rain and the bumpy dirt road, we managed to make progress toward our destination.
    - Manage to + 動詞原形: 強調克服困難的過程
    - How did you manage to finish the project on time?
    - We made progress. => 我們取得了進展。（平鋪直敘）
    - We were able to make progress. => 我們能夠取得進展。（強調有這個能力或客觀條件允許）
    - We managed to make progress. => 我們歷經千辛萬苦，最後還是設法取得了進展。（強調克服障礙後的成功）

## to get better at + V-ing / Noun
- to improve your ability to do something.
- I want to get better at speaking English with native speakers.
- She is practicing every day to get better at playing the piano.
- He is trying to get better at cooking so he can prepare his own meals.
- I hope to get better at time management this year.

## to get the hang of something / V-ing
- to begin to understand how to do something.
- 意思是「抓到……的訣竅」、「掌握……的要領」或「漸漸上手 / 熟練」
- Don't worry if it is confusing at first; you will get the hang of it soon.
- It took me a few days, but I finally got the hang of driving a manual car.
- She is just starting her new job and trying to get the hang of the software.
- Once you get the hang of using natural idioms, speaking English becomes much easier.
- I’m slowly getting the hang of ordering food in English.
