B: What about you? What is your pet peeve?
A: My is when people chew their food with their mouth open. The sound is terrible.

# words
## pet peeve 
- something small that annoys you or makes you angry.
- What is your biggest pet peeve?
- My pet peeve is noisy people.
- My pet peeve is rude people.
- Definitely one of my pet peeve.
- Fears and Pet Peeves.
- It is a pet peeve for her.
- The categories are Fears and Pet Peeves.
- What are your pet peeve? What stresses you out?

## it drives me crazy
- It makes me very angry or annoyed
- It drives me crazy when my phone is slow.
- Bad traffic drives me crazy.

## I can't stand
- I really don't like something or someone.
- I can't stand waiting in long lines.
- I can't stand rude people.
