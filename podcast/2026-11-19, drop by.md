## drop by
> To visit someone for a short time, usually without staying long. Sometimes the visit is not planed.

- I will drop by your house after work.
- My friend dropped by to say hello.

## stop by
> To go to a place for a short visit before going somewhere else.

- I need to stop by the bank this afternoon.
- We stopped by the supermarket on our way home.

## Difference between 'drop by' and 'stop by'

- Both expressions mean to visit a place for a short time. In everyday English, they are very similar.

- Drop by is more often used when visiting a person.
- Stop by is more often used when visiting a place before going somewhere else.

- My friend dropped by to see me, and then we stopped by a coffee shop.

## Pick someone up
- To go somewhere in your car and take someone to another place.

- I will pick you up at 6 pm.
- My father pick me up from school.
