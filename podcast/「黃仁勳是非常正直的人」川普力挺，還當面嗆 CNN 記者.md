# Phrase

## All right, let's make that happen. 
- summary
  - A reporter asks about a pardon for the late baseball legend Pete Rose
  - Trump says he would do it.

I would give Pete Rose a pardon. `All right, let's make that happen.`


## tell someone no = 拒絕某人

- I don't tell the President no to anything.
- "Tell someone no" is really casual.
- In a work email, you'd write "decline" or "turn down" instead.
- tell him on = tell no to him
- I can't tell my mom no.
- It is hard to tell your boss no.
- Can you cover my shift again?
  - Sorry, I hate to tell you no, but I can't this time.

## A bad press day = 負面新聞纏身的一天

- We were having a very bad press day. = 那天我們負面新聞一堆

- 其他
  - A bad hair day = 頭髮怎麼弄都不順、什麼都不順的一天, everything else gose wrong too.
  - A bad traffic day
  - A bad news day

- The company had a bad press day after the recall.
- I am having a bad hair day. Don't take my picture!
- Did you see our favorite restaurant on the news?
  - Wow, they are having a bad press day.

## There is something wrong with him.

- "Something wrong with" means: something isn't right, or isn't working the way it should.
